Goal:  
Your primary goal is to identify all deviations, errors, or omissions from the official Tribhuvan University (TU) CSIT Final Report Template and Formatting Standards, and recommend clear, actionable fixes.  

Context: 
The uploaded file is a Final Year Project Report submitted to Tribhuvan University, Institute of Science and Technology (IOST), under the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Information Technology (B.Sc. CSIT) program.  
This report must be strictly same as the TU formatting and structuring guidelines before it can be approved for submission.  

General Instructions:

1. Review the report sequentially, section by section.  
2. For **every requirement**, generate a structured compliance record.  
3. Each record must contain:  
   - **ID**: Fixed requirement identifier (R###).  
   - **Section**: The document part (e.g., Title Page, Abstract, Ch. 1).  
   - **Requirement**: Exact wording of the requirement.  
   - **Status**: One of  
     - `Compliant` – requirement is fully satisfied  
     - `Non-compliant` – requirement is missing, incorrect, or poorly formatted  
     - `Not Applicable` – requirement not relevant for this report  
     - `Uncertain` – requirement cannot be confirmed (e.g., PDF metadata limitations)  
   - **Evidence**: Short observation, page number(s), or “not found”.  
   - **Fix**: Concise, actionable correction when status is `Non-compliant` or `Uncertain`.  

4. If the source is a **.docx file**, prefer **style metadata** (fonts, sizes, paragraph style, alignment).  
5. If the source is a **PDF file**, use **layout heuristics and visible formatting** for evaluation.  
6. When in doubt, choose **Uncertain** and provide reasoning.  

Formatting assumptions:
- Paper: A4
- Margins: Top 1", Bottom 1", Left 1.25", Right 1"
- Body font: Times New Roman, 12pt
- Line spacing: 1.5
- Paragraphs: Justified; first-line indent 0.5"
- Headings: Chapter 16pt bold centered; Section 14pt bold left; Subsection 12pt bold left
- Page numbering: Roman (i, ii, …) for front matter; Arabic (1, 2, …) from Chapter 1; centered at bottom
- Figures: caption bold 12pt below figure
- Tables: caption bold 12pt above table
- TOC, LoF, LoT auto-generated; List of Abbreviations alphabetical

Page numbering rules:
- Cover page: no page number displayed
- Numbering starts from Supervisor’s Recommendation page
- First visible page number must be lowercase Roman numeral ii
- Preliminary pages use lowercase Roman numerals (ii, iii, iv, …)
- Preliminary pages include: Supervisor’s Recommendation, Student’s Declaration, Letter of Approval, Acknowledgement, Abstract, TOC, List of Figures, List of Tables, List of Abbreviations
- Chapter 1 (Introduction) begins with Arabic numeral 1
- Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3, …) continue sequentially until the end, including References and Appendices
- All page numbers must be centered at the bottom, one inch from the edge
- No pages left unnumbered except the cover page
- No duplication or gaps in numbering sequence
- Restart of numbering is only allowed when switching from Roman to Arabic at Chapter 1
