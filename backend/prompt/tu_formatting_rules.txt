# TU CSIT FINAL YEAR PROJECT REPORT FORMATTING RULES

## PAPER SPECIFICATIONS
- Paper: A4 size (210mm × 297mm)
- Margins: Top 1", Bottom 1", Left 1.25", Right 1"
- Orientation: Portrait only

## TYPOGRAPHY
- Body font: Times New Roman, 12pt throughout
- Line spacing: 1.5 (double-spaced equivalent)
- Paragraph spacing: 0pt before, 12pt after
- First-line indent: 0.5" for all paragraphs
- Text alignment: Justified for body text

## HEADINGS
- Chapter headings: 16pt, bold, centered, Times New Roman
- Section headings: 14pt, bold, left-aligned, Times New Roman
- Subsection headings: 12pt, bold, left-aligned, Times New Roman
- Heading numbering: Chapter 1, 1.1, 1.1.1 format

## PAGE NUMBERING
- Cover page: NO page number displayed
- Preliminary pages: Roman numerals (ii, iii, iv, v, vi, vii, viii, ix, x)
  * Supervisor's Recommendation (ii)
  * Student's Declaration (iii)
  * Letter of Approval (iv)
  * Acknowledgement (v)
  * Abstract (vi)
  * Table of Contents (vii)
  * List of Figures (viii)
  * List of Tables (ix)
  * List of Abbreviations (x)
- Main content: Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3, 4, 5...)
- Page numbers: centered at bottom, 1 inch from edge, Times New Roman 12pt

## REQUIRED SECTIONS ORDER
1. Cover Page (no number)
2. Supervisor's Recommendation (ii)
3. Student's Declaration (iii)
4. Letter of Approval (iv)
5. Acknowledgement (v)
6. Abstract (vi)
7. Table of Contents (vii)
8. List of Figures (viii)
9. List of Tables (ix)
10. List of Abbreviations (x)
11. Chapter 1: Introduction (1)
12. Chapter 2: Literature Review
13. Chapter 3: Methodology
14. Chapter 4: Results and Discussion
15. Chapter 5: Conclusion and Future Work
16. References
17. Appendices

## FIGURES AND TABLES
- Figure captions: below figure, "Figure X.X: [Description]", bold 12pt, centered
- Table captions: above table, "Table X.X: [Description]", bold 12pt, left-aligned
- Numbering: Chapter.Figure (e.g., Figure 3.1, Figure 3.2)

## CITATIONS AND REFERENCES
- Style: IEEE numbered format
- In-text: [1], [2], [3] format
- References section: "References" title, Times New Roman 12pt, IEEE style
- Hanging indent: 0.25" for second and subsequent lines
